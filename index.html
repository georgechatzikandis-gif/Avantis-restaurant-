Our Story

Where the Sea Meets Your Table

"A special place, highly recommended"

Nestled steps from the beach in Afantou, Avantis Fish Restaurant brings the true taste of the Aegean to your plate. With fish delivered fresh every day and a passion for authentic Greek flavors, every meal here is a genuine experience.

Our team, led by the ever-welcoming Manoli, prides itself on warm hospitality and exceptional service. Pick your own freshly speared grouper, savour our signature Symi shrimp saganaki, or simply enjoy the sea breeze over a cold Mythos.
